Live OpenStreetMap feed for real nearby restaurants. Two data tiers: solid pins are ~50 national chains plus NYC-area chains (Just Salad, Chopt, DIG, Pret A Manger, The Halal Guys, &pizza, Fresh&co, Bluestone Lane, Fields Good Chicken) with real published nutrition. Dashed "avg" pins are everything else — independents and unmatched chains — scored on cuisine-average typical-dish nutrition (from public data, by OpenStreetMap cuisine tag), not that restaurant's actual menu; a nutrition API (e.g. Nutritionix) is the only way to get their real dish-level data. Chain figures are representative; validate against official nutrition guides before production. Prices are representative national averages (menu.com / chain nutrition-portal listings) and vary by location and over time — a live pricing API would be needed to track them exactly. Dish icons show food type, not real photos — per-dish photography needs a licensed image source.
